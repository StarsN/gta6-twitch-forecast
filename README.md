Forecasts how many people will watch Grand Theft Auto VI live on Twitch in each hour of its release day (19 November 2026, UTC), as five quantiles per hour, from the first hour's viewer count. It learns the shape of a launch day from the release-day curves of past console launches in the case file, and adjusts that shape for the launch's scale: a launch already huge in hour 1 has less headroom left than a small one.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_INPUT_DIR=input MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
